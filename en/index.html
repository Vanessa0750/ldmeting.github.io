About Us

Enping Lingdong Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. (LDMETING) is a professional audio equipment company integrating R&D, production, and sales. Focusing on conference microphones, digital conference systems, conference speakers and other products, we have a complete production chain and professional technical team. Our products are sold worldwide, providing stable, clear, and professional conference audio experiences for global customers.
