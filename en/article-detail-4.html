Lingdong Invites You to Guangzhou｜2025 Guangzhou Pro Audio & Lighting Exhibition, Hall 2.1 Booth G50

Date: 2025-02-05 Category: Product News Views: 178

The 2025 Guangzhou International Professional Lighting and Sound Exhibition, one of the largest and most influential annual events in the Asia-Pacific audio-visual industry, will be held from May 27-30 at the China Import and Export Fair Complex.

As a recognized platform for business matching, technical exchange, and brand promotion, this year's exhibition brings together thousands of quality brands from domestic and international markets, covering professional audio, conference systems, lighting equipment, and full industry chain solutions.

Exhibition Information – We Look Forward to Your Visit

Exhibition Name: 2025 Guangzhou International Pro Audio & Lighting Exhibition

Exhibition Dates: May 27-30, 2025

Booth Location: Hall 2.1, Booth G50

Venue: China Import and Export Fair Complex

From May 27-30, join us in Guangzhou to experience our premium audio products and explore cooperation opportunities. Lingdong has always been committed to the conference audio field, driven by technological innovation and quality, dedicated to providing stable, efficient, and professional conference audio products and services to global customers.

Lingdong awaits you at Hall 2.1, Booth G50 – see you there!
